Bowen Today

Lunch, weather, what to wear, and news from the principal and PTO

Put it on your home screen and it opens with one tap, like an app.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (in Chrome, it is at the top right).
  2. Scroll the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Arrow’s face appears on your home screen as Bowen Today.

On an Android phone

  1. Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

On a computer

Bookmark this page, or open it on your phone to add it to the home screen.

Open Bowen Today

Apple and Google don’t let a web page add itself to the home screen, so it takes these two taps. The icon then opens Bowen Today directly.
